gba_wifi_link
=============

rfu.c (GBA Wireless Adapter emulation) and serial_proto.c (Pokemon and Advance
Wars link-cable protocol emulation) are from gpSP (gameplaySP),
https://github.com/libretro/gpsp, written by David Guillen Fandos
<david@davidgf.net>, copyright (C) 2023 and (C) 2025. They are unchanged from
the gpSP commit the gpSP core is pinned to (gpsp_HASH in
workspace/tg5040/cores/Makefile) with workspace/all/cores/patches/gpsp/
001-rfu_disconnect_fix.patch and 002-rfu_queue_size.patch applied, except that
each includes gwl_shim.h instead of gpSP's common.h.

gwl.c reproduces gpSP's netpacket glue (libretro/libretro.c) and its random
number generator (main.c, gameplaySP copyright (C) 2006 Exophase
<exophase@gmail.com>). gwl_mode.c reproduces gpSP's per-game link mode
choice (gba_memory.c), and gwl_games.h is generated from gpSP's game table
(gba_over.h).
